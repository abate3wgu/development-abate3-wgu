Entertainment

Relax, explore, and enjoy everything from beaches and rainforest adventures to nightlife and family activities.

Popular Activities

Most people visit Taniti to enjoy the beaches, explore the rainforest, and visit the volcano. However, there are many other things to do throughout the island.

  • Visit a local history museum
  • Go on chartered fishing tours
  • Snorkel in coastal waters
  • Try zip-lining in the rainforest
  • Visit several pubs, including a microbrewery
  • Dance at a new dance club
  • See a movie
  • Take helicopter rides
  • Play at an arcade
  • Visit art galleries
  • Go bowling

Merriton Landing

Many of these activities are located in Merriton Landing, which is a rapidly developing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay.
